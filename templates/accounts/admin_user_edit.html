⚔️ How Your Pixel Wallet Works
🪙 Monthly Initial Allocation

When you add a habit to your routine, your wallet is immediately credited with:
Daily Pixel Score × Pending Days in Month.
e.g. On the 1st (30 days pending): 2 × 30 = 60 Px. On the 18th (12 days pending): 2 × 12 = 24 Px + 5 × 12 = 60 Px = 84 Px total.

🛡️ Daily Defense & Missed Deductions

If you miss completing a scheduled task on any day, you permanently lose that task's daily score from your wallet (e.g. -2 Px or -5 Px lost per missed day).
Consistency protects your balance against decay.

⚡ Keep Your Wallet Energized

You are fighting to keep your pixel wallet solid at 100% Energy without losing pixels. Completing tasks on a daily basis is what keeps your wallet solid, vibrant, and fully energized.
