6. How Your Pixel Wallet Works

1. Initial Monthly Credit At the start of every month (or when a habit is created), the wallet is credited with an upfront pixel reserve: Allocated = Daily Score × Pending Days in Month
2. Daily Dedication & Retention Completing your scheduled habits secures your allocated pixels. Every completed day preserves your 100% Solid Energy without any deductions.
3. Miss Deductions Failing to complete a scheduled day deducts the daily score from your active balance: Lost = Missed Scheduled Days × Daily Score
4. Routined Next-Month Rollover At the end of the month, retained pixels roll forward and combine with your next cycle's fresh allocation: Next Balance = Preserved Pixels + Next Month Allocation
